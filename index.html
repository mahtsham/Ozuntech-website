Common questions

Questions we often hear.

What does a software and AI consultancy do?

We work out which technology solves your business problem, whether that is AI, custom software, a mobile app, cloud infrastructure or better data, and then design, build and support it.

Can you help if we're not sure whether we need AI or custom software?

Yes. Our process starts with discovery, where we listen to the challenge and recommend the right approach before any building begins.

Do you work with startups and established businesses?

Yes. We work with startups and enterprises across industries including healthcare, finance, e-commerce, education, real estate, logistics and retail.
