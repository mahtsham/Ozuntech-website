Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ozun Technologies (operating this website as ozuntech.com; "we", "us") handles when you visit ozuntech.com or contact us, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. If you use the enquiry form or email us at hello@ozuntech.com, we receive what you include: typically your name, email address, the service you are interested in, and your message.
  • Nothing automatically through our own tools. Our website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it does not ask you to create an account.

How the enquiry form works

The form does not send your details to a server we run. When you submit it, your email app opens with your message ready to send to us. Nothing reaches us until you send that email, and it then travels through your own email provider.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project.
  • To keep a record of our conversation while we work with you or consider working with you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third parties involved in running the site

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you load the site, under its own privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare provides our domain name (DNS) service.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on our pages. Your browser requests them from Google, which can see your IP address and browser details, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Your and our email providers handle the messages we exchange.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting work, and for a reasonable period afterward for our records. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@ozuntech.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may also have additional rights under local law, and you may be able to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our services are for businesses. This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, for example by adding analytics, we will update this page and its date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@ozuntech.com.
